Aya and Valentino

I

If you ask me how many times you came to my mind,

I would say only one time, because you arrived and never left.

I think about you more than I can explain, in the big moments

and also in the smallest ones.

I think about you while I eat

while I shower,

while I do my university homework,

while I walk, while I rest,

and even in those seconds when apparently

I am not thinking about anything.

Because somehow,

it is always you.

Sometimes I wish I could stop time just to stay with you for a moment,

to look at you and tell you without words everything my heart keeps inside for you.

I miss you more than I say,

more than I can sometimes show,

and there are days when your absence feels especially strong.

But even when I miss you,

one thing never changes:

every day I love you more.

With you, my heart stopped searching.

I don't want another story or another person.

You are my last woman, Aya,.

the one I want to love for the rest of my life.

I want to share my days, my dreams,

my joys, my worries, and my silences with you.

I want us to look back one day

and know that we never stopped choosing each other.

You are in my thoughts when I wake up,

in my heart when I smile,

and in my dreams when I sleep.

I miss you.

I think about you.

I love you.

And if life gave me the choice again,

I would still choose you,

again and again,

for the rest of my life.
